# Beliefs

These are working intellectual propositions, not slogans that every page must repeat.

- A city is behaviour at scale.
- A boundary on a map is rarely a boundary in reality.
- Growth is not the same thing as development.
- Good architecture begins before the first line is drawn.
- A beautiful building can still be a badly designed system.
- Form is an argument.
- Precision is a form of care.
- Infrastructure is choreography made physical.
- The most expensive failure may happen outside the project.
- Maintenance is part of design.
- Technology does not remove complexity. It gives complexity another surface.
- A model is useful when it changes a decision.
- The best digital layer may be the one nobody notices.
- Capital follows confidence, and confidence follows understanding.
- A development is never only an asset.
- Sequencing is strategy.
- People will use the system they actually have, not the one in the presentation.
- Observe again.
