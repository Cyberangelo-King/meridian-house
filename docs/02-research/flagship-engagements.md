# Flagship Engagements

All engagements in this prototype are fictional.

1. **Lagos Civic Exchange** — civic and public-space system.
2. **Aba Production City** — industrial and production ecosystem.
3. **Makoko Water Edge Study** — waterfront and settlement interface.
4. **Kigali Growth Corridor** — growth and movement system.
5. **Accra Civic Commons** — civic/public realm.
6. **Montréal Climate Housing Block** — climate-resilient housing study.
7. **Toronto African Innovation District** — innovation and diaspora ecosystem.
8. **Lekki Logistics & Coastal Systems Plan** — logistics, coastline, and infrastructure dependencies.

Each engagement should eventually document:

- ambition
- what the client brought
- what Meridian discovered
- disciplines involved
- key question
- intervention
- evidence
- consequence
- what changed
