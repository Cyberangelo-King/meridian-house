# Visitor Research

## Visitor questions

- Who is Meridian?
- What does Meridian actually do?
- Is this architecture, consulting, research, or all three?
- How do they approach complex projects?
- Can they handle uncertainty?
- What evidence do they produce?
- Where does my problem fit?
- How do I start?

## Conversation routes

1. We have an ambition.
2. We have a site.
3. We have a project in motion.
4. We have a question.
5. We need an independent view.
6. We want to collaborate.

The website should answer these questions without forcing every visitor through the same conversion path.
