# 00 — Client

## Practice

Meridian House is an independent multidisciplinary practice working across architecture, spatial design, urban strategy, research, intelligence, advisory, and technology-informed modelling.

Its strongest public proposition is the combination of **architecture/design with research and intelligence**.

## Buyers

Primary audiences include governments and cities, foundations, development organisations, infrastructure companies, universities, real-estate developers, and investors.

Secondary audiences include journalists, researchers, conference organisers, policy thinkers, technology companies, architects, designers, and high-intent readers.

## Business model

A client may arrive with an ambition such as a new building, district, civic project, development, infrastructure intervention, research question, or strategic uncertainty.

Meridian does not treat the requested deliverable as the whole problem. It studies the system around it, coordinates the relevant disciplines and specialists, tests consequences, documents the reasoning, and equips the client to act.

## Experience objective

The website should feel like entering an institution that thinks carefully before it speaks.

Desired emotional sequence:

**Curiosity → orientation → discovery → understanding → trust → possibility**

## Important content boundary

Meridian House is a fictional institutional world for this build and case-study exercise unless a real client later approves equivalent claims.

Invented projects, publications, partnerships, awards, metrics, quotations, history, personnel, clients, and credentials must not be presented as verified real-world facts.
