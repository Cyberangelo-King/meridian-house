# Client Brief

## Core brief

Create an institutional website for Meridian House that communicates a multidisciplinary approach to African urban and architectural problems without becoming a conventional consultancy brochure.

## Requirements

- Establish Meridian House as a serious institutional practice.
- Make the relationship between architecture, research, intelligence, modelling, and advisory visible.
- Turn methodology into an experience rather than a generic services list.
- Give selected work enough context to demonstrate how the practice thinks.
- Create a clear route from intellectual interest to conversation.
- Make the immersive layer subordinate to meaning, accessibility, performance, and SEO.
- Preserve a reusable architecture for future content and case studies.

## Non-goals

- Generic corporate landing-page patterns.
- A personal portfolio disguised as an institution.
- Decorative 3D without narrative purpose.
- Unsupported claims of regulated professional services.
